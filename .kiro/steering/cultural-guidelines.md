# Cultural Guidelines

This project portrays a living (if endangered) art form and traditional Indian stories. Every piece of generated copy, art direction and animation must follow these rules.

## Tone
- Spooky comes from the setting (an abandoned theatre at night, a guttering lamp, dust, drifting ghost lights) and from the spooky tales themselves. Never from mocking or distorting the art form or its people.
- There is no ghost narrator or ghostly performer character. The user is the puppeteer.
- Panchatantra tales are playful and wise: keep their morals intact and their animals charming.
- Ghost and demon tales (Punchkin, Kolli Deyyam) should be eerie and atmospheric, never gory. The audience includes kids (8+).

## Story accuracy
- Retell each tale faithfully in broad strokes, simplified for short, shadow-play scenes. Verify plots against reliable sources before writing story specs.
- **Punchkin:** in the original, the prince tears the parrot apart. Our version softens this: the parrot is freed, the spell breaks and the sorcerer crumbles to dust.
- **Kolli Deyyam:** this is a folklore figure, not a fixed canonical tale. Our story is original and inspired by the folklore, and the credits must say so.
- Ramayana content is Phase 2 only. Do not add epic characters in Phase 1.

## The art form
- Name it correctly: Tholu Bommalata (Andhra Pradesh / Telangana). Related forms: Togalu Gombeyata (Karnataka), Tholpavakoothu (Kerala), Thol Bommalattam (Tamil Nadu).
- Puppet art should echo the real style: translucent painted leather, perforated detailing, hinged limbs, bold reds/yellows/greens/blacks. Do not copy any specific artist's puppet; create original designs inspired by the style.

## Credit
- The ending screen and the README must credit Tholu Bommalata and its performer communities, the Panchatantra, and *Old Deccan Days* (Punchkin), and point users to learn more.
- Never imply the project is affiliated with or endorsed by any performer family or institution.

## Assets
- Only original art or assets with a clear license. No copyrighted music. Record or generate original sounds (mridangam heartbeat, lamp crackle, night crickets, wind).
